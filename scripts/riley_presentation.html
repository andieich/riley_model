Model equations

Differential Equation

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d} P}{\mathrm{d} t } = (\overline{P_h} - R - G)~P \]

, where \(P\) is the Phytoplankton biomass, \(\overline{P_h}\) the average photosynthisis in euphotic zone, and \(G\) the grazing rate.

Photosynthesis Rate

The biomass-specific photosynthesis rate \(\overline{P_h}\) is calculated as

\[ \overline{P_h} = \frac{\textcolor{#B3541E}{p}~\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{I_0}}{\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{k~z_\text{euph}}}~(1-\mathrm{e}^{-\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{k~z_\text{euph}}})~\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{L_\text{N}~L_\text{V}} \]

Parameters are shown in red and forcing variables in blue

Parameters:

  • \(\textcolor{#B3541E}{p}\) = 2.5 \(\mathrm{\frac{gC~gC^{-1}~d^{-1}}{g~cal~cm^{-2}~min^{-1}}}\) Photosynthetic rate per unit irradiance

Forcing variables:

  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{I_0}\): Light at seasurface in \(\mathrm{\mu E~m^{-2}~s^{-1}}\)
  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{k}\): Light attenuation in the euphotic zone\(\mathrm{m^{-1}}\)
  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{L_\text{N}}\): Limiting effects of turbulence, unitless
  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{L_\text{V}}\): Limiting effects of nutrients, unitless
  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{z_\text{euph}}\): Depth of the euphotic zone in \(\mathrm{m}\)

\(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{L_\text{V}}\) is calculated as

\[ \textcolor{#2B5F9E}{L_\text{V}} = \min(1, \frac{\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{z_\text{euph}}}{\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{z_\text{mix}}}) \] , where \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{z_\text{mix}}\) is the depth of the mixed layer in \(\mathrm{m}\)

\(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{z_\text{euph}}\) is calculated as

\[ \textcolor{#2B5F9E}{z_\text{euph}} = \log(\frac{\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{I_0}}{\textcolor{#B3541E}{I_\text{min}}}) \] , where \(\textcolor{#B3541E}{I_\text{min}}\) is a parameter defining the light intensity at the base of the euphotic zone: 0.0015 \(\mathrm{g~cal~cm^{−2}~min^{−1}}\)

Respiration Rate

The biomass-specific respiration rate \(R\) is calculated as

\[ R = \textcolor{#B3541E}{R_0}~\mathrm{e}^{\textcolor{#B3541E}{r}~\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{\text{temp}}} \]

Parameters:

  • \(\textcolor{#B3541E}{R_0}\): 0.0175 \(\text{d}^{-1}\) Respiration rate of phytoplankton at 0 ◦C, in \(\mathrm{d^{-1}}\)
  • \(\textcolor{#B3541E}{r}\): 0.069 \(\text{°C}^{-1}\) Temperature dependency of respiration rate

\(\textcolor{#B3541E}{r}\) is equal \(Q_{10} = 2\), i.e. doubling of rate for 10 °C increase in temperature: \(\mathrm{e}^{10 \text{°C}~0.069~\textrm{°C}^{-1}}\approx2\)

Forcing Variables:

  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{\text{temp}}\): Surface (?) seawater temperature in \(\textrm{°C}\)

Grazing Rate

The biomass-specific grazing rate \(G\) is calculated as

\[ G = \textcolor{#B3541E}{g}~\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{zoo_{\text{bm}}} \]

Parameters:

  • \(\textcolor{#B3541E}{g}\): 0.0075 \(\mathrm{\frac{d^{-1}}{gC~m^{-2}}}\) Clearance rate of grazers

Forcing Variables:

  • \(\textcolor{#2B5F9E}{zoo_{\text{bm}}}\): Biomass of zooplankton in \(\mathrm{gC~m^{-2}}\) (?)

GAMs to interpolate forcing

As seen in Figure 2, some predictions are quite ‘jaggy’. This is probably due to the linear approximations used for the forcing variables. Additionally, using the next value for timepoints outside of the range of the forcing data set (start = -15 days, end = 365 days) is not ideal.

As an alternative, use “cyclic” GAMs. Cyclic means here, that the first values are considered when fitting the last within a period of 365 days.

Although GAMs are fitted only once outside of the model function, the prediction step takes much more time than approx().
